Our story

Finest quality. Fair prices. Proudly Australian.

Liberation Botanicals was born from 12 years in wellness and a simple belief: the finest quality shouldn't cost a fortune.

We spent those years developing brands and building retail presence, learning what works and what customers truly want. We took that experience, and the relationships we built across manufacturing, and set out to make products the way we always believed they should be made: thoughtfully, honestly and without unnecessary markups.

Every product is made in Australia with local manufacturers we know and trust, and scented the way nature intended. Soft, authentic and never synthetic.
